The **Exoplanet Sonic Bestiary** is an interactive web application exploring the sonification of known exoplanetary systems using data from the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Each planetary system is translated into sound from its physical and orbital properties. The orbital period determines the fundamental frequency of each planet, while its mass controls its relative intensity. Orbital eccentricity modifies the timbre, and orbital inclination is translated into three-dimensional binaural spatialization.

The host star is represented by spectrally coloured noise related to its effective temperature, while its rotation period produces a characteristic double-pulse envelope. The evolution of each system is accelerated to make orbital motion perceptible on human timescales, with a temporal compression automatically adapted to the shortest orbital period while remaining adjustable by the listener.

The catalogue is automatically updated from the **NASA Exoplanet Archive**, allowing known planetary systems to be explored through both their astronomical parameters and their sonic representation.
